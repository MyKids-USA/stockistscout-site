The terms, in plain words

20% for 12 months

Of what each customer actually pays us, after tax and refunds. Monthly and annual plans both count.

A 60-day link

Your link is remembered for 60 days on the visitor’s device, so a brand that reads first and signs up later is still yours.

Earned after 30 days

Each payment counts once it is 30 days old and not refunded. A refund that comes later is taken off your next payout.

Paid on the 15th

Ask for a payout from $100 by PayPal, Zelle or ACH, or from $300 by international wire (the wire fee comes out of the payout).

What does not count

Customers on the launch offer (Plus at the Basic price) earn no commission, and neither do your own account, company or cards.

Tax forms

If you earn $600 or more in a calendar year, we need a signed W-9 (US) or W-8BEN (outside the US) before paying. You upload it on your partner page, where it is stored encrypted.
