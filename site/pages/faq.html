The service

Who is StockistScout for?

For businesses that sell to stores: brands, wholesalers, makers and suppliers who want more stores carrying their products. It is B2B only.

Do you email consumers?

No. Your agents write only to stores and businesses, at their business contact addresses. StockistScout is not for selling to the public or for consumer marketing email.

Which countries do you send to?

At launch, only to recipients in the United States. Your business can be elsewhere, but the stores contacted will be in the US. Other countries will follow once we can do it as well, and within their laws.

What are the agents?

AI agents, each specialised in one kind of store. When you describe your ideal customer, the agent for that store type is assigned. See Meet our agents.

Do you guarantee sales or orders?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We stand behind the work: stores that match what you described, careful reviewed emails, sent within limits. Whether a store buys depends on your product, your price, your offer and your conversation with them.

When does it open?

Soon, first come, first served. The waitlist is open now: join it and we will write when there is room for you.

Your emails

Who signs the emails?

Your business. The sender name is your business name and the reply-to is your own email. The email goes out from an address on our dedicated sending domain, so your own domain’s reputation is not put at risk. When a store replies, it writes to you.

Can I see and change what is sent?

Yes. You see the template and every store contacted on your dashboard, and you can edit the template at any time. Any new or edited template goes through review before it sends.

What does template review check?

That it is honest, uses no other companies’ brands or names, makes no false claims, has what CAN-SPAM asks for (a real sender, a postal address and an opt-out) and does not read like spam. An AI (Claude) reviews it automatically and approves it, returns it with changes, or, when in doubt, passes it to a person on our team. Nothing sends on an unapproved template.

Is template review legal advice?

No. The service is built to follow CAN-SPAM, and automated review helps catch problems, but it is not legal advice and does not guarantee that an email meets every law that applies to you. What your emails offer, and that it is true, remains your responsibility. For legal questions, ask your own lawyer.

Are opens accurate?

Not entirely. Apple Mail and many security scanners open emails automatically to protect their users, so an “open” is not always a person reading. That is why the dashboard leads with qualified stores, positive replies and conversations started, and keeps opens as a secondary, approximate figure.

How many emails go out a day?

Few at first, on purpose: that is how the emails reach the inbox. Every account starts at a cap of 25 a day and rises 50% each healthy week (bounces under 2% and no complaints), up to 500 a day. Starter and Plus reach their volume in the first month; Growth around week 4, Pro around week 6 and Enterprise around week 9. If bounces go above 2% or a complaint arrives, sending pauses by itself. See the ramp, plan by plan.

Can a store be emailed again and again?

No. A store gets one email and, if it does not reply, a single follow-up 7 days later. After that it gets nothing more through StockistScout, from any customer, for 90 days. And anyone who unsubscribes with the one-click link never hears from you through us again.

Data

What data do you need from me?

What the questionnaire asks: your business name and website, your industry, your products and wholesale prices, the email where you want replies, a postal address for the email footer (CAN-SPAM requires one), your ideal customer and your offer. We do not need your customer list or access to your mailbox.

Where do the stores come from?

From public business information: store websites and other public business sources. We use business contact details only, never personal ones. See the privacy policy.

Do you sell data?

No. We do not sell your data or the stores’ data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Price

How much does it cost?

The trial is 50 emails, once, free; if you start straight on a plan, those 50 are added to your first month. Plans run from Basic (200 emails a month, $49.99) to Enterprise (15,000 emails and 8 agents, $1,199.99). Billed monthly, cancel anytime. See pricing.

Do you take a commission?

Never. Just the monthly plan.
