The service

Who is StockistScout for?

For businesses that sell to stores: brands, wholesalers, makers and suppliers who want more stores carrying their products. And also for those who sell to companies: a hotel looking for corporate groups and events, or a service business looking for corporate clients. It is B2B only.

Does it work if I sell to companies, not stores?

Yes. In “Your business” you choose who you sell to: stores or companies. With companies, your agents look for corporate clients, groups, event planners and teams traveling to your area, near the cities you choose, and never your competitors. The offer questions change to what a company needs to know: who qualifies, billing, how they can see you first, and an introductory offer.

Will you help me fill in the setup?

Yes. At the end of each section there is a “Review with Katya” button: she reads what you wrote and suggests improvements field by field, which you accept or not. She can also write your emails (the first one, a version B and the follow-up) and fix what the template review asks for. Each new review uses one assistant message from your plan; seeing again a review of something that has not changed is free. More about Katya.

Do you email consumers?

No. Your agents write only to stores and businesses, at their business contact addresses. StockistScout is not for selling to the public or for consumer marketing email.

Which countries do you send to?

To recipients in {{COUNTRIES:en:and}}. You choose the country for each agent: an agent works one country and writes in its language, so to reach both you use two agents. The Caribbean islands are one choice: one agent works all six. Belgium is two choices: Brussels and Wallonia in French, Flanders in English. Your business can be anywhere. In Canada we follow its anti-spam law (CASL), and in Australia and New Zealand their equivalents (the Spam Act 2003 and the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007). In the United Kingdom (PECR) we only write to limited companies, LLPs and Scottish partnerships, checked at Companies House, never to sole traders. In France (CPCE L34-5, CNIL) we only write to businesses whose trade fits the offer, and every email says where the address came from and how to object (GDPR). Guadeloupe and Martinique are part of France and get the same French rules. In Peru, Colombia, Panama and Guatemala we only write to a company inbox (info@, ventas@), never to a person's address, and emails to Peru, Colombia, Panama and Guatemala say PUBLICIDAD in the subject (Peru's Ley 28493, Panama's Ley 51 art. 85); emails to Colombia go out only Monday to Friday 7am-7pm and Saturday 8am-3pm, Bogotá time (Ley 2300). In Argentina emails also say PUBLICIDAD in the subject and carry the withdrawal notice of its Disposición 4/2009. In Honduras and Paraguay we write in Spanish, only to a company inbox, never to a person's address. In Paraguay the subject and the first line say “Comunicación comercial no solicitada”, as its Ley 4868/2013 requires, and the footer says the address came from the store's website. In Ireland we only write to companies (S.I. 336/2011), and in Belgium only to a company's generic inbox such as info@, in Brussels and Wallonia (Royal Decree of 4 April 2003); both carry the GDPR notice. In Singapore subjects start with <ADV> (Spam Control Act), and in Hong Kong we only write to a company's generic inbox, with the sender and unsubscribe lines also in Chinese (UEMO, PDPO). In Brazil we write in Brazilian Portuguese, only to a company inbox (contato@, vendas@), never to a person's address, and every email carries an LGPD notice saying where the address came from and how to object; stores there can be on Shopify or Nuvemshop. On the Caribbean islands (Trinidad and Tobago, the Bahamas, Barbados, the Cayman Islands, Bermuda and Aruba) we write in English, and one agent works all six, placing each store on its island from its own website; in Barbados we only write to a company inbox, with a privacy line in the footer. In Israel (Communications Law, section 30A) emails are in English, and the first one is a single, neutral request for consent, with no links or prices. Only a store that answers yes gets your email, marked “פרסומת” (advertisement) as the law requires; a no, or no answer, means we never write to that store again. There is no follow-up, and nothing is sent on Shabbat or Israeli holidays. Stores there are on Shopify (Wix soon). In Norway, Finland, Estonia, Lithuania, Iceland, Croatia and Flanders we write in English, only to a company's generic inbox (info@, post@, salg@), never to a named person, and only to companies, never to sole traders; every email carries the GDPR notice and the sender's contact line, and a reply STOP unsubscribes. In India, the Philippines, the United Arab Emirates and Jordan we write in English, only to a company inbox, never to a person's address; in the Philippines every email also carries a privacy notice saying who sends it, that the address came from the store's website and how to object (Data Privacy Act). In Morocco we write in French, only to a company inbox (Law 09-08). In South Africa (POPIA, section 69) emails are in English, and the first one is a single, neutral request for consent, which says where the store's address was found. Only a store that answers yes gets your email; a no, or no answer, means we never write to that store again. There is no follow-up. In Taiwan we write in Traditional Chinese by default (or in English, if you choose). Every email says, in Chinese and English, where the address came from and offers a free way to refuse, and a refusal, by the link or a reply 「不要再寄」 (“don’t send again”), stops the emails of every brand at once (Personal Data Protection Act, articles 19 and 20). Sites that say 「謝絕推銷」 (“no solicitation”) are skipped. Stores there can be on any platform, including SHOPLINE, CYBERBIZ and 91APP. In these thirty-two countries (Chile included, Ley 19.496 art. 28 B) and on the Caribbean islands we only write to business addresses that are published without a notice refusing commercial messages, and every email shows how to reach the sender and unsubscribe.

Do you guarantee sales or orders?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We stand behind the work: stores that match what you described, careful reviewed emails, sent within limits. Whether a store buys depends on your product, your price, your offer and your conversation with them.

Can I start today?

Yes. Create your account (with your email or Google) and you get a free trial of 50 emails, no card needed. Fill in your business, your ideal customer and your offer, your template is reviewed, and you launch your agent.

Data

What data do you need from me?

What the questionnaire asks: your business name and website, your industry, your products and wholesale prices, the email where you want replies, a postal address for the email footer (CAN-SPAM requires one), your ideal customer and your offer. We do not need your customer list or access to your mailbox.

Where do the stores come from?

From public business information: store websites and other public business sources. We use business contact details only, never personal ones. See the privacy policy.

Do you sell data?

No. We do not sell your data or the stores’ data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Pricing and billing questions are on Pricing. What the service does (review, sending limits, dashboard) is on Features; the agents, on Agents.
