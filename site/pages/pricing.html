The sending ramp: why your plan does not go out on day one

A new sender that sends a lot at once ends up in spam, and your name with it. So every account starts at a cap of 25 emails a day, which rises 50% each week up to 500 a day. The ramp climbs only after a healthy week: bounces under 2% and no complaints. If not, it holds where it is until it is.

A plan’s emails are for its month, so in the first month a large plan cannot use them all: when you start, a smaller plan and moving up as the ramp climbs is usually the better deal. Billing questions

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No, never. You pay the monthly plan and nothing else. The orders you win are yours, in full.

What counts as an email?

Each email your agent sends to a store. Finding stores, drafting, review and the dashboard do not use up emails.

What happens when I reach the month’s limit?

Your agents stop sending until your plan’s next month starts, or until you move up a plan. You are never charged extra without asking for it.

Why don’t all my emails go out on day one?

Because daily limits start low and ramp up gradually, to protect the deliverability of your emails and every other customer’s: 25 a day in week one, 50% more each healthy week, up to 500 a day. Starter and Plus fit in their first month; Enterprise reaches its pace around week nine. See the ramp, plan by plan.

How am I billed?

Monthly, by card. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see your card number.

Can I cancel or change plans?

Yes, whenever you want. No contract: if you cancel, you are not charged again.

Is there a catch to the trial?

It is 50 emails, once per business, at no cost, with the same rules and the same review as any plan.
