About

We built it because we needed it.

StockistScout is made by Aharon & Ita Corp, a company based in Florida, United States: the same team behind MyKids-USA, Drop-USA, Bins-USA and ScanRights.

Our story

Sellers first.

We sell our own products to stores. We know what it takes to find stores that fit and write to them one by one, and how little a generic email does.

So we built agents that look for stores for our own brands every morning. StockistScout turns those agents into a service, with the same rules we use under our own name: review before sending, send few and send well, and respect anyone who says no.

How we work

Three rules that do not change.

01

Your name comes first

Every email carries your business name. That is why we review every template and limit sending, even when it is slower.

02

No promises we cannot keep

We do not guarantee sales, we do not invent numbers, and we tell you when a figure, like opens, is approximate.

03

Respect for the recipient

Businesses only, never the same store twice in 90 days, and a one-click unsubscribe that is always honoured.
