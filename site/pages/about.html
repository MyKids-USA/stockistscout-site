About

We built it because we needed it.

StockistScout is made by Aharon & Ita Corp, a company based in Florida, United States: the same team behind MyKids-USA, Drop-USA, Bins-USA and ScanRights.

Our story

Sellers first.

We sell our own products to stores. We know what it takes to find stores that fit and write to them one by one, and how little a generic email does.

So we built agents that look for stores for our own brands every day. StockistScout turns those agents into a service, with the same rules we use under our own name: review before sending, send few and send well, and respect anyone who says no.

The family behind the agents

Built with technology. Inspired by family.

Our AI agents are named after, and inspired by, our founder’s grandchildren. The technology behind them is artificial intelligence; their names and faces stand for something very human: family, dedication and the wish to build something worthwhile for the next generation.

And to be clear: no children work here. Just AI agents, inspired by the people we love most.
