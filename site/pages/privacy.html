Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

StockistScout (“the service”, “we”) is a service of Aharon & Ita Corp, a company based in Florida, United States. Our agents find stores that may carry our customers’ products and write to them on those customers’ behalf. This policy explains what data we handle, about whom, why, and for how long.

1. Visitors to this website

This website uses no advertising. Your language choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) and never reaches us. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address in order to deliver them.

We use Google Analytics (GA4, by Google) to measure how many people visit the site and how they arrive. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives data about your visit, such as the pages you view, your device and your approximate location. We do not use it for advertising. You can block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or your browser settings.

We also use our own analytics, with no third parties, to improve the site: it records the pages you view, how far you scroll, the links and buttons you click (their visible text only), your device type and approximate country and city, and records the visit so our team can watch it, also live. A visit identifier is kept in your browser only while the tab is open. Form fields are masked: we never keep what you type, and we do not keep your IP address. Recordings are deleted after 7 days.

Your account. When you create an account we keep your name, your email and what you enter in the app (your business, ideal customers, offer and templates) to provide the service. Payments are processed by Stripe: we never see or store your card number. We delete your data when you close your account and ask us to.

If you call our sales line (+1 (401) 417-2688), Katya, a voice agent, answers. We keep a written summary of the call, the number you called from and the details you give us (name, email, company, message), so we can answer you. We use the call for nothing else, and we delete these details if you ask.

2. Our customers

When you open an account, we handle:

  • Account data: your name, your email and your password (stored as a hash, never in plain text).
  • Questionnaire answers: your business details, products and prices, your ideal customer, your offer, the reply-to address and the postal address shown in the emails.
  • Your templates and the outcome of their review.
  • Billing data: your plan and your payments. Card details are handled by Stripe; we never see them.

If you install StockistScout from the Shopify App Store. The app asks for a single permission, to read your store’s products (read_products), so it understands what you sell. On install, Shopify gives us your store’s name, domain, contact email and business address, which we use to fill in your business details; you can change them in the app. We do not read your customers, your orders or your buyers’ data. Your store’s access token is stored encrypted. These accounts are billed by Shopify, on your Shopify invoice. When you uninstall the app, the token stops working and your agents stop; 48 hours later Shopify asks us to erase your store’s data, and we delete your account and everything in it.

3. The stores we contact

To find stores we use public business information, such as store websites and other public business sources. We handle business contact details only: the store’s name, website, location, business email address and, where it is public, the buying contact. We also keep why the store fits a customer and what happens to each email: delivered, bounced, opened, clicked, replied or unsubscribed.

We write only to businesses, never to consumers, and only to recipients in {{COUNTRIES:en:and}}. We use only business contact details the businesses publish themselves (the email on their website and the business name). Anyone in Mexico can ask for access, rectification, cancellation or opposition (ARCO rights) by writing to our contact email. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Anyone who unsubscribes goes on our suppression list and gets no more emails from that customer through us; we keep only what is needed to honour that. Each customer writes to a store once, with at most one follow-up 7 days later (in Israel, a single request for consent, without links, which a store refuses by replying no or by not answering, and no follow-up; only a store that agrees in writing gets the customer’s email); a store receives at most one email through StockistScout every 30 days.

If you received an email from us and want to know what we hold about your business, or want it deleted, email us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} or reply to the email. If we delete it, we keep only your address on the suppression list so we never write to you again.

4. How we use it

  • To find stores that fit each customer’s ideal customer, write to them on that customer’s behalf, and show the results on their dashboard.
  • To review templates, apply sending limits, the 30-day rule and opt-outs, and protect email deliverability.
  • To bill plans and support customers.

We do not sell data, about customers or stores, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who processes it for us

  • Anthropic (AI provider, United States): to find stores, draft and review emails. Under its commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models.
  • Resend (email delivery, United States): delivers the emails and reports deliveries, bounces and complaints.
  • Stripe (payments, United States): processes payments for accounts opened on our website.
  • Shopify (Canada and United States): gives access to the store that installs the app and bills those accounts.
  • xAI (United States): only if you call the sales line. Its voice-agent platform receives the call audio to understand you and produce Katya's voice, and keeps the transcript so we can review it.
  • Twilio (United States): only if you call the sales line. It is the phone carrier that connects the call.
  • Render (hosting, United States): runs the service, its database and this website.

6. Retention

A customer’s data is kept while the account is active and deleted within 90 days after it is closed (accounts installed from Shopify, when Shopify asks after the uninstall), except billing records the law requires us to keep. For stores, we keep business contact details while they are useful to the service and delete them on request; the suppression list and the minimal record behind the 30-day rule are kept as long as needed to honour them.

7. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access is limited to the people who operate the service. No system is perfect; if an incident affected you, we would tell you as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it. Depending on where you live, the law may give you other rights; we honour them. We will answer within the legal time limits.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell customers by email.
