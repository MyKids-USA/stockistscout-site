How it works

From your description to a store that replies, in five steps.

You say what you sell, to whom, and on what terms. Your agent searches, writes and sends within limits that do not bend. You see every step and answer whoever replies.

01

Answer the questionnaire

It is detailed on purpose: the better you describe your business and your customer, the better the stores your agent finds and the better it writes to them. You can change your answers at any time.

Your business
Name, website, industry, products and wholesale prices.
Your ideal customer
Store type, niche, area, size, signs that a store fits, exclusions, and 3 to 5 example stores.
Your offer
Minimum order, payment terms, samples, an opening discount and what you want the store to do next; plus the tone and language of the emails.
02

Tune and approve

Before anything is sent, your agent shows you 10 sample stores with the reason each one fits. You approve or correct them, and the search is tuned from that.

Then comes the email template. Every template, new or edited, stays “Pending approval” until it passes review. If something needs to change, it comes back with the changes requested.

  • Honest: no false claims or promises you cannot keep.
  • No other companies’ brands or names.
  • Built to follow CAN-SPAM: a real sender, a postal address and an opt-out.
  • Nothing that reads like spam.
  • Doubtful cases are checked by a person on our team.
  • Automated review helps, but it is not legal advice.
03

Daily sending, within limits

Every day your agent finds new stores and writes each one its own email, under your business name, from our dedicated sending domain. The limits protect your name and the deliverability of every customer.

  • A daily cap that starts low and ramps up gradually.
  • Automatic pause if bounces go above 2% or complaints come in.
  • One email and a single follow-up per store; then 90 days of quiet, across all customers.
  • One-click unsubscribe in every email, honoured for good.
  • At launch, recipients in the United States only.
04

Your dashboard

Every store contacted, with why it fits, and what happened next. From there you pause or resume an agent and edit the template; an edited template goes back to review before it sends.

  • Qualified stores found, with the why for each one.
  • Positive replies and conversations started: what really counts.
  • Clicks, as a signal in between.
  • Opens, as a secondary, approximate figure: Apple Mail and some scanners inflate them.
  • Unsubscribed, so you know who wants no more email.
05

Replies land in your inbox

Your email is the reply-to address. When a store answers, it writes to you, in the mail app you already use. From there the conversation, the samples and the first order are yours, with no commission.

What it does not do

So you know what to expect.

  • It does not guarantee sales. It finds stores that fit and writes to them well. Whether they buy depends on your product, your price and your conversation.
  • It does not email consumers. Stores and businesses only. It is not a tool for selling to the public.
  • It does not mass-send. The daily limits and the 90-day rule cannot be skipped, even when your plan has emails left.
  • It does not leave the United States, for now. At launch it only writes to recipients in the US.
