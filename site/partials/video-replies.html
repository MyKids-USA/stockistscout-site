Video replies by Katya

When a store replies, Katya answers with a 30-second video.

When a store answers your agent’s wholesale email with interest or a question, Katya, our AI avatar, makes that store a 30-second video on behalf of your brand. She greets the store by name, shows two of your own products that suit it, says why, and gives your wholesale terms: minimum order, payment terms and first-order discount. Your agent sends it as one more email in the same thread.

  • The video page’s button goes to your own wholesale link, or asks the store to reply to the email. A small footer reads “Video made by StockistScout”.
  • Videos in English and Spanish.
  • Each agent has its own On/Off switch. Turn the add-on on in the app, under Plan & billing.
  • A video that can’t be made, or is no longer wanted (the store unsubscribed or said no), is never charged.
