The problem

Landing new stockists is hours of work almost nobody has time to do well.

  1. 01Finding stores that fit is slow. Maps, social media, store websites one by one, and then working out who to write to.
  2. 02Writing to each one well takes even longer. A generic blast gets ignored or lands in spam. A personal note for every store does not scale.
  3. 03The usual shortcuts cost you. Commission on every order, a platform’s cut, lists that go stale, or a domain burned by mass sending.

How it works

You describe your ideal customer. Your agent does the rest, and you see all of it.

  1. Questionnaire

    Your business, your products and wholesale prices, the kind of store you want, where, and what you offer: minimums, terms, samples.

  2. Your agent finds stores

    Every day it looks for stores that match your description and notes why each one fits.

  3. Personal emails, reviewed

    Each store gets its own email on behalf of your business. Every template is reviewed before a single email goes out.

  4. Your dashboard

    Who was contacted and why, opens, clicks, replies and unsubscribes. Replies go straight to your own inbox.

Trust

Your name is on every email. So we guard it.

These rules come with every plan, and they cannot be switched off.

  • Every template is reviewed before it sends. A new or edited template stays “Pending approval” until it passes review: honest, no other companies’ brands or false claims, nothing spammy. Doubtful cases go to a person on our team.
  • Never the same store twice in 90 days. A store that got an email through StockistScout gets no other, from any customer, for 90 days.
  • One-click unsubscribe. Every email carries an unsubscribe link. Anyone who unsubscribes never hears from you through us again.
  • CAN-SPAM compliant. Honest sender and subject lines, a postal address, and opt-outs honoured.
  • Your business name and your reply-to. The email carries your business name, and when a store replies, it writes to you.
  • Daily limits that ramp up. Sending starts low and grows over time, and pauses by itself if bounces or complaints rise.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Who do the emails go to?

Only to stores and businesses, never to consumers. At launch, only to recipients in the United States.

Who signs the emails?

Your business. The email goes out under your business name, from an address on our dedicated sending domain, with your own email as the reply-to: when a store answers, it writes to you.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. We stand behind the work: stores that match what you described and careful emails, sent within limits. Whether a store buys depends on your product, your price and your conversation with them.

Are opens accurate?

Not entirely, and the dashboard says so. Apple Mail and some security scanners open emails on their own, so opens come out inflated. Clicks, and above all replies, are the real signal.
