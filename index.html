The problem

Landing new stockists is hours of work almost nobody has time to do well.

  1. 01Finding stores that fit is slow. Maps, social media, store websites one by one, and then working out who to write to.
  2. 02Writing to each one well takes even longer. A generic blast gets ignored or lands in spam. A personal note for every store does not scale.
  3. 03The usual shortcuts cost you. Commission on every order, a platform’s cut, lists that go stale, or a domain burned by mass sending.

Why it is different

The usual ways to reach stores, and what they really cost.

Each of them works for someone. Here is what they charge and what they leave to you.

The optionHow you payThe catch

Sales reps on commission

How you payA percentage of every order, for as long as the account keeps ordering.

The catchThey carry many lines at once and cover one territory. You do not see who they called or what they said.

Wholesale marketplaces

How you payA cut of every order that goes through the platform, often of reorders too.

The catchYou sit in a catalog next to every other brand, on terms the platform sets. The relationship is the platform’s, not yours.

Contact databases

How you payA subscription or a price per list.

The catchYou get names, not stores that fit. Choosing, writing and sending is still your job, and the data goes stale.

Mass-email tools

How you payPer contact or per send.

The catchYou bring the list, the copy and the judgment. The same email to everyone shows, and your domain’s reputation is on you.

StockistScout

How you payA flat monthly plan by emails sent. No cut of your orders, ever.

The catchAn agent finds stores that fit, says why, writes each one a personal email, reviewed and sent within daily limits, and the replies come to you. No sale is guaranteed: the conversation and the close are still yours.
