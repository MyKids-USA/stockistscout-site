Meet our agents

Eight agents.
Each one knows one kind of store.

Our agents are AI agents. Each one specialises in one kind of store: it knows what to look for to decide whether a store fits what you sell, and how to write to the person who buys for it.

  • Tammy Baby & kids’ clothing stores

    Looks for children’s boutiques and baby stores whose range and price point fit your line, and writes to each one about why it belongs on their shelves.

  • Elias Gift, home & décor shops

    Finds gift shops, home stores and décor boutiques that carry products like yours, and introduces your brand to the person who buys for them.

  • Raquel Fashion & accessories boutiques

    Searches for fashion and accessories boutiques whose style and prices match yours, and writes to each one personally.

  • Roberto Wholesalers, distributors & warehouses

    Finds wholesalers, distributors and warehouses that could carry your line in volume, and opens the conversation on your behalf.

  • Rony Sports, outdoor & adventure stores

    Looks for sporting goods, outdoor and adventure stores where your products fit, and tells each one why.

  • Gabriel Beauty, personal care & wellness

    Finds beauty, personal-care and wellness shops that suit your products, and writes each one a note about what you can offer them.

  • Aaron Pet stores

    Searches for independent pet stores and pet boutiques that fit your line, and introduces your products to them.

  • Joel Toys, games & hobby shops

    Finds toy stores, game shops and hobby stores that match your range, and writes to each one about your products.

The agents are artificial intelligence. Their names and characters are inspired by our founder’s grandchildren. Our story

How they are assigned

You describe your ideal customer. The specialist shows up.

The kind of store you want to reach decides it. If none of the eight fits, create your own.

  1. Describe your ideal customerIn the questionnaire: the kind of store you want to reach.
  2. The specialist is assignedThe agent who specialises in that kind of store takes your ideal customer: its own search, its own emails and its own report on your dashboard.
  3. Add more as you growEach ideal customer gets its agent. Basic, Starter and Plus include 1, Growth 2, Pro 4, Scale 5, Business 6, and Premium and Enterprise all 8.

Every agent follows the same rules: stores only, reviewed templates and sending limits. The rules, on Features.
