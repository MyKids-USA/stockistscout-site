How it works at one hotel

1

The manager describes the customer

Which companies matter (sports teams, productions, companies with projects nearby, weddings, conferences), in which cities, and what the hotel offers. Katya helps write it.

2

The agent searches every night

It finds real companies with a contact email on their own website, a second review drops the ones that do not fit, and the emails go out in the morning.

3

It writes with approved templates

A first email and one follow-up, from the hotel's own sending domain. Never to a company that unsubscribed.

4

The manager closes the booking

Replies reach the manager's inbox, and the reports show what works.

What the chain gets

One shared do-not-contact list

Two hotels of the same chain never write to the same company, and an unsubscribe at one hotel counts for all of them.

A corporate dashboard

Every hotel on one page: companies found, emails sent and replies, by hotel and by manager.

A sending domain per hotel

Each hotel sends from its own domain, warmed up step by step. One hotel never affects another hotel's delivery.

The brand's voice

Templates approved before anything is sent, so every manager speaks the same way.
