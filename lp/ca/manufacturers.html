Questions

Can I reach US stores too?

Yes. Each agent works one country: one agent for Canada and another for the United States. Your business can be anywhere.

What does it cost?

The trial is free: 50 emails, once, no card. Then monthly plans by emails sent, from $49.99 a month for 200 emails, in US dollars. No commission, no contract, cancel anytime.

Do you guarantee sales?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We find stores that fit what you described and write to them carefully; whether a store buys depends on your product, your price, your offer and your conversation with them.

Where do the replies go?

To your own inbox: your email is the reply-to. From there the conversation, the samples and the first order are yours.

Which stores do you write to?

Stores and businesses only, never consumers, at business addresses published on their own websites or other public business sources. Each agent works one country: Canada, the United States, Mexico and more than 30 other countries.

Do I need Shopify?

No. You can sign up on our website with your email or Google and pay by card. If you sell on Shopify, you can bring in your best sellers to show in your emails.
